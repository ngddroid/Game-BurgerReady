Privacy Policy
Bun Voyage! · Last updated: 23 November 2026

🍔The short version

Bun Voyage! is an offline burger-tycoon game. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your progress is saved only on your device.

No accountNo ads (yet)No analyticsWorks offline

1Who we are

This game is made by NG Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at support@ngstudio.example.

2Information stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves a small file on your phone containing:

  • levels unlocked, stars and shift results;
  • in-game currencies (Bucks and Gems), upgrades, Stores, skins, pets and themes;
  • daily reward and mission progress;
  • your settings (music, sound, haptics and graphics quality).

This file never leaves your device through us. It may be included in your personal Android backup (managed by Google under your own account settings).

3Information we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No advertising ID and no tracking across apps.
  • No payment data (the game has no real-money purchases at this time).

4Permissions

The game only uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can switch off any time in Settings → Haptics.

5Ads and purchases in the future

If we add advertising or in-app purchases in a future update, we will update this policy before that version is released, ask for your consent where the law requires it, and update the Google Play Data safety section.

6Children

Bun Voyage! is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Progress, or uninstall the app, to delete everything the game stored on your device.

8Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
